- Card estimates are explicitly derived as 80%-120% of verified final hammer bids, rather than represented as published auction estimates. The method and original values are in sources.json.
- Non-GBP figures use the fetched 16 October 2026 Frankfurter rate. Converted estimates and results are rounded to whole GBP.

- Catalogue condition, ownership, signatures and game/screen use are source attributions. No physical inspection, independent authentication, regrading or watch timekeeping test was performed.
