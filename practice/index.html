Consignment Tool

Add one practice item in around five minutes. Use an ordinary item you own. It will not be listed for sale.

For Windows. A Mac or no-install alternative is available by replying to Steve.

  1. Download the Consignment Tool and check the publisher against the name shown here.
  2. Install and open it. Add item details, one photo and an approximate asking price in pounds.
  3. Click “Save practice listing”, then reply to Steve when the saved message appears.

The signed Windows installer is not available yet. Publisher and supported Windows versions have not been verified. File size and checksum will be added with the verified release.

Works offline. Your practice entry stays on your device.

If Windows shows “unrecognised app”, or the publisher does not match, stop and email stevelikejobs@proton.me. Do not bypass the warning.

Prefer no installation? Reply to Steve and we will use a short call: describe one ordinary item and the price you would record for it. There is no penalty for choosing this route.
