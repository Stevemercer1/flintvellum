# FlintVellum collection

The site retains the captured Christie’s homepage layout, component styles, Arizona fonts, header and footer structure. FlintVellum’s supplied monogram and wordmark replace the house branding. The homepage now uses the supplied watch, card and memorabilia photographs and records throughout. Company information remains in company.html. Careers has its own page at careers/, linked from the homepage and shared navigation. Both roles prefer UK or EU candidates, welcome international candidates and require UK-hours overlap. The role titles, salary lines, recruitment contact and mailto are unchanged.

The supplied archive contains 250 closed lots: 100 watches, 100 trading-card lots and 50 memorabilia lots. All 947 original image files are copied without modification. Each lot has a static page under lots/, with its full gallery, specifications, condition, provenance, original catalogue link and source notes. The catalogue supports category, keyword, maker, auction, verified-hammer and saved-lot filters, sorting and pagination. Saved selections stay in localStorage on the visitor’s browser. Gallery controls support keyboard, touch and an enlarged view.

Every record is labelled closed. None is offered for bidding or represented as FlintVellum custody. There are 127 verified hammer results and 123 unverified public hammers. Card ranges are explicitly derived from verified results, not described as source estimates. Converted figures retain the supplied FX date and original values. The corrections and competing catalogue dates in the collection report remain visible on the relevant lot pages. Unknown years stay unknown.

The input records are preserved under data/lots.json, data/sources.json and data/REPORT.md. Raw collection pages, staging files and duplicate evidence were not copied. Images are local under img/. Public source records are accessible from archive.html. Account registration, live bidding, payments and consignment submission have no backend in this static site; no controls claim those transactions work.

The original publisher’s trackers are detached. FlintVellum’s existing fingerprint.js is unchanged. Exactly one script hook appears immediately before the closing body tag on every generated page. Existing source photographs and video remain in the repository’s historical asset collection but are no longer referenced by these pages. The source asset manifest remains in clone.json.

The approved settlement policy is stored in docs/site-copy.json and AGENTS.md. Buyers can settle in Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDC or USDT through escrow, with other major assets considered on request. Seller payouts are in GBP or USDC. The homepage and company page share the approved third consignment step; both hero subtitles name Bitcoin and Ethereum explicitly.

Rebuild with `python3 docs/populate.py`. To import a replacement supplied dataset, run `python3 docs/populate.py ../auction-data`. This runs docs/rebrand.py against the saved christies-pristine Git tag before populating the current archive. Running rebrand.py alone only regenerates the earlier unpopulated homepage.

`node docs/verify.cjs --company` covers the careers route, homepage consignment section and their mobile navigation. `node docs/verify.cjs` exercises the site in Firefox under a /flintvellum/ path prefix. It covers desktop and narrow layouts, category filters, search, paging, price sorting, saved state across pages, mobile navigation, gallery controls, unknown years and the reported source conflicts. Screenshots and results stay in ignored docs/verify-shots/. `python3 docs/verify-data.py` covers every generated route, local asset path, original image hash and the protected role/contact copy.
