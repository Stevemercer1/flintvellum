# Founder About

My name is Stefan Mercer, although I’ve gone by Steve since my teens. I’m Swiss, born near Geneva, and now in my early forties.

I started dealing watches in my early twenties. Small margins, long hours. I learned references, movements, condition and provenance because getting any of them wrong cost money. A watch had to stand up to inspection.

Dealing led to sourcing. Private clients wanted particular pieces, often references that were nowhere on the market. My job was to find them, check them and get the deal done discreetly. “Unavailable” usually meant I had more calls to make.

That reputation took roughly fifteen years to build. Fifteen years of phone calls, introductions and keeping my word. Collectors, dealers, estates and auction rooms across Europe and Asia became people I could call. Eventually, I became the person they called when the usual routes had run out.

I got into crypto early because my clients did. I watched collectors comfortable with digital assets struggle to consign a valuable watch somewhere that understood crypto settlement. Too often, the payment method became the story instead of the object.

I built FlintVellum to make that straightforward. Authentication, custody and settlement belong in one considered process. A collector consigning a five-figure watch should know who is inspecting it, where it is held and how they will be paid.

I spent years finding the right watch. Now I’m building the place I would trust to sell it.

Steve Mercer · Founder, FlintVellum
