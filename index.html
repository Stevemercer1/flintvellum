The consignment process

From collection to consignment.

  1. Tell us what you have.

    Share the item details and photos. A specialist reviews the item, discusses an estimate and agrees a reserve with you.

  2. We inspect and catalogue it.

    We arrange insured collection, authenticate the item and hold it in secure custody while its timed auction runs.

  3. The buyer pays. You get paid.

    The buyer settles in USDC, USDT or ETH through escrow. Your GBP or USDC payout follows delivery and the inspection window.

Prices are in GBP. Our standard buyer’s premium is 15%, with no seller commission. Shipping, insurance and applicable taxes are disclosed separately.

Our specialisms

Three categories. Specialist attention.

Collectible watches

Mechanical wristwatches, with specialist checks of the reference, movement, condition and provenance before auction.

Graded trading cards

Sports and Pokémon cards, with slab, grading-record and provenance checks. Existing grades are verified, not replaced.

Memorabilia

Sports and film memorabilia, with specialist inspection of the item, its provenance and supporting authentication records.

The house

An auction house, with the details handled.

FlintVellum auctions authenticated collectibles across watches, graded trading cards, and sports and film memorabilia, with specialist inspection, physical custody and crypto settlement. Collectors consign items; we handle inspection, cataloguing, bidding and settlement.

Founded in 2022, we are a 40-person, Series A team with a London operations hub and colleagues working remotely. Our next step is practical: more auction dates, better settlement tools and stronger relationships with sellers.
